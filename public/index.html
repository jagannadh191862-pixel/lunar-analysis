Planetary Optics & Multi-Modal Correspondence

1. Chandrayaan-2 Instrument Specifications

The ISRO Chandrayaan-2 orbiter carries two complementary optical imaging instruments operating from a nominal 100 km polar orbit:

TMC-2 (Terrain Mapping Camera-2)
• Spatial Resolution (GSD): 5.0 meters / pixel
• Swath Width: 20 km (panchromatic triplet stereo)
• Primary Role: Regional 3D digital elevation modeling and crater topology mapping.
OHRC (Orbiter High Resolution Camera)
• Spatial Resolution (GSD): 0.32 meters / pixel
• Swath Width: 12 km (at 100 km altitude)
• Primary Role: Ultra-high-resolution hazard mapping, boulder detection, and micro-crater characterization.

2. Geometric Transformation & RANSAC Formulation

Planetary image registration models the mapping between the reference frame $x_A \in \mathbb{R}^2$ and target frame $x_B \in \mathbb{R}^2$ through an 8-DOF Projective Homography:

$$\begin{bmatrix} x_B \\ y_B \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \sim \begin{bmatrix} h_{11} & h_{12} & h_{13} \\ h_{21} & h_{22} & h_{23} \\ h_{31} & h_{32} & h_{33} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_A \\ y_A \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

The consensus inlier set is iteratively formed by sampling minimal 4-point subsets and calculating the symmetric transfer error: $$\epsilon_i = \|x_{B,i} - H x_{A,i}\|_2 \le \tau_{\text{RANSAC}}$$
